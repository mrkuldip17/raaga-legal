# Raaga — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 23, 2026**

## 1. Information We Process
Raaga may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, command inputs, and information required to operate its features.

## 2. How Information Is Used
Information may be used to respond to commands, manage music queues, maintain security, and troubleshoot errors.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention
Raaga may use a database to store information needed for its features. The exact information stored and its retention period depend on the bot's actual implementation. The developer will ensure that this policy accurately describes the bot's real data practices.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Raaga uses Discord and may rely on other third-party services to provide its features. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Requests
Users may contact the Raaga developer through the bot's support channel to ask about their data or request deletion.

## 6. Policy Updates
This policy may be updated when Raaga's features or data practices change.

## 7. Contact
For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Raaga developer through the support channel provided with the bot.
